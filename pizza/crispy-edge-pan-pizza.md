# Crispy Edge Pan Pizza

Detroit-style pan pizza with a thick, airy crust and deeply caramelized, crispy cheese edges (frico). Inspired by the LetsCookie YouTube short.

**Source:** [LetsCookie – crispy edge pan pizza](https://youtube.com/shorts/BB7-g4Vb_bI)

**Yield:** 1 large rectangular pan pizza (serves 4–6)  
**Active time:** about 30 minutes  
**Rise:** 2–3 hours (or overnight cold ferment)  
**Bake:** about 12 minutes at 550°F

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 350g bread flour (2½ cups)
- 227g water (1 cup), lukewarm
- 9g fine salt (1 tsp)
- 2.5g instant yeast (¾ tsp)
- 7.5g olive oil (1½ tsp)
- 5g granulated sugar (1 tsp)

### Sauce

- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp fine salt
- ½ tsp granulated garlic
- 2 tsp granulated sugar
- 2 Tbsp olive oil

### Toppings

- Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- Low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella, shredded (generous amount; Grande recommended if available)

### For the pan

- 2–3 Tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. **Activate the yeast.** In the bowl of a stand mixer, combine the water, sugar, and instant yeast. Stir and let sit about 10 minutes until foamy.

2. **Mix the dough.** Add the bread flour, salt, and olive oil. Knead with the dough hook 8–10 minutes (or by hand) until smooth and elastic.

3. **Bulk rise.** Lightly oil a large bowl. Form the dough into a smooth ball, place seam-side down in the bowl, cover tightly with plastic wrap, and let rise until doubled (1½–2 hours at room temperature, or overnight in the fridge for better flavor).

4. **Prepare the pan.** Lightly coat a 10×14-inch Lloyd Detroit-style pan (or similar deep rectangular pan, or a well-seasoned cast-iron skillet / 9×13 cake pan) with 2–3 tablespoons olive oil.

5. **Shape.** Transfer the dough to the pan and gently stretch it toward the edges. Cover and let rest/proof 30–60 minutes until puffy.

6. **Top.** Spread a thin, even layer of sauce, leaving about a ½-inch border around the edges. Sprinkle generously with freshly grated Parmesan, then cover with a generous amount of shredded low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella. Push cheese all the way to the edges of the pan so it touches the sides (this creates the crispy caramelized edge).

7. **Bake.** Bake at 550°F for about 12 minutes, or until the cheese is golden brown and the edges develop a deep, crispy caramelized crust. (Adjust time if your oven or pan runs cooler.)

8. **Cool and serve.** Let cool 1–2 minutes before carefully removing from the pan, slicing, and serving.

## Notes

- The sauce makes more than needed for one pizza. Freeze the extra for future batches.
- Grande whole-milk mozzarella is excellent if you can source it (often available at GFS or Restaurant Depot). Low-moisture mozzarella is key for good browning and less sogginess.
- For maximum crispy cheese edge, make sure the cheese contacts the pan walls all the way around.
- A dark, heavy pan (Lloyd pan, cast iron, or dark metal) gives the best crust. Lighter pans may need a few extra minutes or a higher rack position.
- Optional: cold ferment the dough overnight for deeper flavor.
- This style is very close to classic Detroit pan pizza.
