# Garlic & Herb Tangzhong Rolls

Savory adaptation of a milk-bread dinner roll. Same tangzhong starter and pull-apart 8×8 pan method, with less sugar, garlic and herbs in the dough, and garlic-herb butter after the oven.

**Yield:** 9 rolls (8×8 pan, about 3 inches deep)  
**Active time:** about 30 minutes  
**Rise:** about 3 hours total  
**Bake:** 20–25 minutes at 350°F

## Ingredients

### Milk paste / tangzhong

- 45g milk (~¼ cup)
- 45g water (~¼ cup)
- 15g AP flour (2 Tbsp)

### Dough

- 325g AP flour (2⅔ cups)
- 15g sugar (1 Tbsp)
- 5g yeast (1 tsp)
- 110g warm milk, about 86°F (½ cup)
- 8g kosher salt (1½ tsp)
- 1 egg
- 60g softened butter (3 Tbsp)
- 2–3 garlic cloves, finely minced (or ¾ tsp garlic powder)
- 1½ Tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tsp finely chopped fresh thyme or rosemary (or ½ tsp dried Italian seasoning)
- Optional: 15g finely grated Parmesan (~2 Tbsp)

### Egg wash

- 1 egg + 1 Tbsp milk, stirred together

### Garlic herb butter

- 45g butter (3 Tbsp)
- 1–2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 Tbsp chopped parsley
- Pinch of salt
- Optional: pinch of chili flake or extra Parmesan

## Method

1. **Make the tangzhong.** Bring the 45g milk and 45g water to a simmer. Whisk in 15g flour. Cook, stirring constantly, about 30 seconds until smooth and thick. Cool 10 minutes.

2. **Mix the dough.** In a stand mixer bowl combine warm milk, yeast, sugar, egg, flour, salt, and the cooled tangzhong. Mix on medium-low until combined.

3. **Add butter.** Add softened butter a little at a time until it disappears into the dough.

4. **Add garlic and herbs.** Mix on high about 1 minute. Add minced garlic, herbs, and Parmesan if using. Mix just until evenly distributed.

5. **First rise.** Cover and rise on the counter about 90 minutes.

6. **Shape.** Divide into 9 balls, about 75g each (they may run a gram or two heavier with the add-ins). Roll tightly and place seam side down, 3×3, in an 8×8 pan.

7. **Second rise.** Cover and proof about 90 minutes, or until about 2–2.5× larger.

8. **Bake.** Brush with egg wash. Bake at 350°F for 20–25 minutes, until well browned.

9. **Finish.** While they bake, melt the finishing butter with garlic just until fragrant (do not brown unless you want a nuttier taste). Stir in parsley and salt. Brush the hot rolls as soon as they come out of the oven.

## Notes

- Sugar is cut from 50g in the sweet milk-roll version to 15g so the rolls taste savory.
- Fresh garlic in the dough is milder after baking; most of the punch comes from the butter on top.
- Fresh garlic can slightly slow the rise. Give it the full 90 minutes, or a little more if the dough looks sluggish.
- For a sweeter garlic profile, roast a small head of garlic and mash 3–4 cloves into the dough instead of raw minced.
- Same pan, proof times, and bake as the original milk rolls.
