# Strawberry Vanilla Golden Oreo Dirt Cake

No-bake layered dessert inspired by classic dirt cake. Crushed Golden Oreos for the “dirt,” a dense cream cheese layer, strawberry pie filling, and a vanilla pudding–whipped topping.

**Yield:** 10–12 servings (9×13 pan or large trifle)  
**Active time:** 25 minutes  
**Chill:** at least 4 hours, ideally overnight

## Ingredients

### Cookie layer

- 1 (14–16 oz) package Golden Oreos, crushed (reserve ½–¾ cup for topping)

### Cream cheese filling

- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### Strawberry layer

- 1 (21 oz) can strawberry pie filling (use 2 cans for a thicker fruit layer)

### Pudding topping

- 2 (3.4 oz) boxes instant vanilla pudding mix
- 3½ cups cold milk
- 1 (8–12 oz) container whipped topping, thawed

### Optional garnish

- Fresh strawberries, sliced or whole
- Extra crushed or whole Golden Oreos
- White chocolate shavings

## Method

1. **Crush the cookies.** Pulse Golden Oreos in a food processor, or bag them and crush with a rolling pin, until they look like coarse sand. Set aside ½–¾ cup for the top.

2. **Make the cream cheese filling.** Beat softened cream cheese and butter until smooth, 2–3 minutes. Add powdered sugar and vanilla and beat until fluffy.

3. **Make the pudding topping.** Whisk pudding mixes with cold milk about 2 minutes until it starts to thicken. Fold in whipped topping until smooth. It will firm as it sits.

4. **Layer.** In a 9×13 pan, large trifle bowl, or individual cups:
   - Spread about two-thirds of the crushed cookies on the bottom and press lightly.
   - Spread the cream cheese filling over the cookies.
   - Spoon strawberry pie filling over the cream cheese. If it is very stiff, warm it briefly or loosen with a spoonful of juice.
   - Spread the pudding topping over the fruit.
   - Finish with reserved cookie crumbs and fresh strawberries.

5. **Chill.** Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, so the layers set.

## Serving

Scoop from the pan or spoon from a trifle so each serving gets cookie, cream cheese, strawberry, and topping. Individual cups or mason jars show the layers cleanly.

Keeps covered in the fridge 3–4 days. Do not freeze; the cream cheese layer can separate.

## Notes

- For a denser, more classic dirt-cake texture, fold part of the cream cheese filling into the pudding instead of keeping them fully separate.
- Strawberry instant pudding can replace vanilla if you want more berry flavor throughout the topping.
- Fresh strawberries tossed with a little sugar can stand in for pie filling if you want a lighter, less-sweet fruit layer.
- Homemade whipped cream works in place of store-bought whipped topping.
